01 · About me

A love for baking,
a joy in creating.

I am a professionally trained pastry chef working across celebration cakes, chocolate, breads, pastries and desserts — with every product made 100% eggless.

My experience comes from two kitchens. At Cakes N Creams Baking Academy in Mohali, I worked on customised customer orders, taking each creation from preparation to final finish while maintaining quality, consistency, and attention to detail. In June 2023, I founded Eternal Blaze Bakeshoppe by Divya, an independent home bakery that I run myself, handling everything from product development and baking to decoration and order management. These experiences have shaped my approach to pastry — combining creativity with practical skills, precision, and a genuine passion for creating thoughtfully made products.
